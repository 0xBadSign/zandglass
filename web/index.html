Pass on your shielded ZEC. Privately.

Shielded Zcash has no timelocks, so there is no private way to pass it on. Zandglass gives a vault a time-locked recovery path: your heir can open it only after you stop checking in, and nothing on chain reveals the plan, the heir or the amounts.

How it works

  1. Your heir makes a key in the browser and sends you a public heir code.
  2. Zandglass moves your funds into a fresh vault and seals its seed twice: to the heir code (age, X25519) and to a future drand round (timelock).
  3. While you are around you flip: funds move to a new vault and a new kit is sealed, so old kits open empty vaults.
  4. If you stop, the sand runs out: the heir opens the kit and restores the vault in any Zcash wallet.

Real, on mainnet

Every funding, flip and claim is an ordinary shielded transaction (default expiry and anchor, ZIP 317 fee, no outgoing viewing key). Mainnet evidence: fund b58f467f..., flip d5ce33a9..., claim e4fb38b6... A live vault unlocks on 2026-11-12. Details in the evidence file.
